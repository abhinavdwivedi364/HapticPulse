## 🚀 Getting Started
HapticPulse is designed to help you maintain focus and manage work while using tactile cues. This tool is especially useful for individuals with neurodivergent conditions, like ADHD, who need extra support during deep work sessions.

### 🖥 System Requirements
- **Operating System:** Windows 10 or later, macOS 10.13 or later or Linux (Ubuntu 20.04 or later)
- **Memory:** At least 4 GB RAM
- **Storage:** Minimum of 100 MB free disk space
- **Other:** A DualShock 4 controller for optimal functionality

### 💾 Installation Steps
1. Once the download completes, locate the file in your Downloads folder. 
2. Double-click the HapticPulse installer file.
3. Follow the prompts to install the software on your computer.
4. After installation, connect your DualShock 4 controller using a USB cable or Bluetooth.
5. Open HapticPulse from your application menu.

## ⚙️ How to Use HapticPulse
1. **Launch the Application:** Start HapticPulse after installation.
2. **Connect Your Controller:** Make sure your DualShock 4 is connected. HapticPulse uses this for feedback.
3. **Set Up Your Work Session:** Choose the duration for your focused work session and start the timer.
4. **Receive Tactile Feedback:** During your session, the controller will vibrate to remind you to stay focused.
5. **Finish Session:** When the timer ends, take a short break and reflect on your productivity.

## 🎯 Features
- **Tactile Feedback:** Get vibrations from your controller to help maintain focus.
- **Customizable Sessions:** Adjust session lengths and feedback intensity.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Navigate easily with intuitive options.
- **Open-Source:** Contribute to the project or modify it to fit your needs.

## 📚 Troubleshooting
If you face any issues:

- Check if your operating system is up to date.
- Restart HapticPulse and try again.
